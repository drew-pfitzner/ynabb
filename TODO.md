## Done

- 2026-10-15: **Weekly debt repayments (Dani).** A loan's settings have 'How often': each month, or every Monday to Sunday (e.g. Simone's ATO plan, $205 every Friday). Its payment category then asks for that amount on every such day in the month, and the budget line shows the next one ('next $205.00 on Fri 16 Oct'). The accounts list and payoff plan use weeks too. Also fixed: adding a loan could make its payment category twice.
- 2026-10-15: **No doubles on the starting balance's day (Dani).** A starting balance is often typed from the bank app late in the day, after that day's spending. Importing then added those purchases again, so the account came out a few dollars short. Import now reads NAB's running balance: the lines that leave exactly the starting balance on that day are skipped, under '… already in the starting balance'.
- 2026-10-15: **Always the same colours (Dani).** The app no longer switches to dark colours when a phone, iPad or Mac is in dark mode, so everyone sees the same budget, bars included. The dark colours are kept in the code but switched off.
- 2026-10-15: **Bar colours on older browsers (Dani).** On older Safari (iPads and Macs not updated since 2022), the budget bars had no green, orange or purple because those browsers skip one kind of colour rule. They now get plain backup colours, as do highlighted transaction rows.
- 2026-10-15: **Weekly targets (Dani).** A new 'Set aside every week' target: an amount and a day (e.g. $50 every Friday). Each month it asks for that amount times how many of that day the month has, so a month with five Fridays asks for five lots.
- 2026-10-15: **A prettier, story-told guide (Dani).** The guide tells Lizzy's 1970 payday story with animated cash, envelopes and calendars: every dollar gets a job, the simple sum, only spending what you've got, big non-monthly costs, rolling with the punches, paying off debt and getting a month ahead. Soft chapter colours, sticky notes and handwritten labels; Back sits at the top. Setup groups envelopes into Needs, Wants and Freedom, and amounts can be weekly to yearly or Custom (a next due date that repeats, saved as a 'payment due on a date' target).
- 2026-10-10: **Reconcile is one tidy list (Drew).** The import review shows a short summary, three numbers and rows grouped, with already-imported lines folded away. While reconciling, the old 'Possible doubles' box and 'Not matched yet' cards are gone: everything is a row with a tick under one heading, so nothing shows twice. Order: Different Amount, then No Matching Bank Transaction (a red cross deletes it, both sides of a transfer; open it to Keep It), then Possible Matches, then the rest. A line under No Matching Bank Transaction says whether deleting them makes YNABB match the bank, with a Delete All or Delete These button when it does.
- 2026-10-10: **Ready to Assign below zero is readable (Drew).** When more is assigned than you have, the red box showed only a "Fix this" button: its amount and words were red on red. They are white now.
- 2026-10-10: **Settings, amounts and phone pickers (Drew).** Settings are grouped cards with one-line rows and much less text, in two columns on wide screens. Money boxes take the dot as you type it (no more cents filling in), with a dot key on the number pad. The transaction sheet drops the ⋯ menu and shows 'Add a bank description' under the payee. Phone pickers show plain background under the list while the keyboard is up.
- 2026-10-10: **No more lost fixes after shipping (Drew).** If fixes were saved on a branch after it went live, session start, /ship and /sync now keep them and move them onto a new branch, instead of deleting the branch.
- 2026-10-10: **Phone tidy-up and transfers to tracking accounts (Drew).** Phone Transactions: long account names end in "…", Import & reconcile sits in a footer row on the account card, the top row is just Search, Filters and +, and Select (then All / Done) sits beside the count. Select mode on a phone for picking several, with a selection bar that fits. Possible doubles show while reconciling, and a typed transfer can merge with the bank's copy. Money moved into a tracking account (super, shares) is spent from a category. Updating a tracking account's value adds one adjustment. Money boxes show commas (1,234.50).
