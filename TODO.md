## Done

- 2026-10-17: **Safety copy before big replaces (Drew).** Restore, Start fresh and Move from YNAB now go to the server in one go. The server saves a copy of the whole budget first, then swaps everything at once, so a dropped connection can't leave half a budget. If it fails, the budget is left as it was and you're told. Settings › Backup and moving gains a "Budget before the last restore" row: download it, then Restore from backup to go back. The last 10 copies are kept.
- 2026-10-17: **Changes no longer vanish on a weak signal (Drew).** A change that can't reach YNABB now stays queued and keeps retrying (faster when the connection comes back) instead of being dropped after two tries. It's also kept in the browser, so closing the page, reloading or being signed out doesn't lose it: it's sent next time YNABB opens. Leaving the page still warns while anything is unsaved.
- 2026-10-17: **Tests for the budget sums (Drew).** 16 tests cover carry-forward, overspending, Ready to Assign, splits, transfers, credit cards, group totals and targets. No bugs found. Note: `overspentBefore` counts one carried overspend again each month, but nothing shows it yet.
- 2026-10-17: **Import no longer skips a real repeat transfer (Drew).** A line with no dd/mm in it (e.g. a $10 transfer on Monday and again on Tuesday) was skipped as "already in" when the new file started after the first one. Now it's only called a re-send when the earlier line's date falls inside the new file's dates but is missing from it. ATM lines name their day and time, so they're still caught either way. The dd/mm rule is left as is: the written date and shop make a real repeat very unlikely.
- 2026-10-17: **ATM withdrawals aren't doubled when they clear (Drew).** NAB writes a withdrawal as "ATM 15/10 09:09 280-282 BANNA AVE GRIFFITH" at first and "NABATM CSH 15th09:09GRIFFITH" once it clears, so NAB Family was out by $100. The day and time now link the two. On import the entry takes the final line (like a pending purchase), and in reconcile the pair shows under Possible Matches.
- 2026-10-17: **Lines with no date in them aren't doubled when NAB re-sends them (Drew).** Re-importing Transactions 8 left NAB Family out by $99.99: the $100 ATM withdrawal and the BrickX $0.01 had both come in again with a new processing date, and Dani's check needs a dd/mm in the line. Now a line with the exact amount, the same words (moved about or cut short is fine) and within 2 days of an earlier import is skipped as already in. A different ATM time or reference counts as a different line. Reconcile also offers several doubles at once when together they explain the gap. A merged transfer keeps your own name ("Cash", not "NAB (ATM)").
- 2026-10-17: **A transfer typed by hand merges with the bank's line, and a tidier selection bar on the phone (Drew).** The BrickX $0.01 transfer had already taken one bank line, then NAB sent it again with the words in a different order, so reconcile didn't offer it and merging said "One is a transfer". Now reconcile offers the pair when the account is out by exactly that much and every word of one line is in the other. Merging keeps the transfer, adds the bank's details and remembers both bank lines. On a phone the selection bar is shorter. The count sits by Done, then the merge button with one short line under it, then Approve and Delete. Payee, category and note sit behind **Change all…**, and nothing runs off the side.
- 2026-10-15: **Reconcile catches a bank line imported twice (Dani).** Reconcile never offered two lines that both came from bank files as a possible double, so Dani's extra Driver Foodworks $4.60 left it out by $4.60 with nothing to fix. Now, when the account is out by exactly their amount and both name the same day and shop, they show under Possible Matches, and one tick merges them. The merged one remembers both bank lines, so later imports skip it.
- 2026-10-15: **A line that comes back reworded isn't doubled (Dani).** NAB can send the same cleared purchase in a later file with a different processing date or wording (Driver Foodworks $4.60, once with a reference number and once without). Import treated it as new. Now, if an earlier import has the exact amount, the same written date and the same shop name within 3 days, the new line is skipped as already in. A second real purchase still comes in when the file has both.
- 2026-10-15: **Pending charges match more forgivingly when they clear (Dani).** A cleared line with the exact amount, the same written date and the same first 6 letters of the shop name now updates the pending one, whatever words follow the name (town, currency, website). TradingView was still showing as new and 'released'.
- 2026-10-15: **Overseas purchases clear properly (Dani).** A pending overseas charge (e.g. 'POS 09/10 TRADINGVIEWA*PRODUCT WESTERVILLE') clears with its foreign amount instead of the town ('… USD 19.99 …'). So import showed it as new and said the pending one had been released. The foreign amount is now ignored when comparing shop names, so the pending one is updated in place.
- 2026-10-15: **Import pairs the closest matches first (Dani).** Bank lines used to be matched to typed-in entries one at a time, top of the file first. So a $4.60 line could take the entry belonging to another $4.60 a few days away, leaving the right one to come in as a double. Now every possible pair is scored first and the closest pairs win across the whole file. Automatic tests cover it.
- 2026-10-15: **Tap a category to move money into it (Dani).** Tapping a category's Available amount now opens Move money with that category under 'Give to'. It takes from its group, or Ready to Assign when there's money there, and fills in what its target still needs. The swap button turns it round to take money out. Overspent categories and Ready to Assign work as before.
